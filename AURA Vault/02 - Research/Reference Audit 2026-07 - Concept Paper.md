## Bottom Line
Of the ~80 references checked: roughly two dozen were clean, ~40 had at least one wrong field (author list, volume/issue/page, or DOI), and 6 could not be verified as real publications at all. The pattern — real DOI/title but fabricated or mismatched author lists — appeared often enough to treat as systemic rather than a handful of typos. Corrected replacement text was produced wherever a real source could be confirmed.
